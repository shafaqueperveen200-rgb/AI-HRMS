Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 17 October 2026

1. Introduction

AI HR Management System is designed to help organizations manage recruitment, interview scheduling, candidate evaluation, and employee information in one place. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the system.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the system is used, it may collect names, email addresses, account credentials, resumes, job applications, interview schedules, evaluation scores, feedback, and employee-related information.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to manage user accounts, process job applications, organize interviews, maintain employee records, and support recruitment decisions through programmed Python logic.

4. Demo and Real Data

The demonstration version uses sample data for testing and presentation. When the system is configured for an organization, it may process real employee and candidate information. Users should not enter real personal or confidential information into the public demo.

5. AI-Based Evaluation

The system may generate resume scores or candidate assessments using programmed logic. These results may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed by authorized HR personnel. Automated scores should not be the sole basis for recruitment decisions.

6. Data Access and Security

Access to personal information should be limited to authorized users according to their roles. Reasonable security measures should be implemented to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Data Storage and Sharing

Information may be stored in the database and hosting environment configured for the system. Personal information should not be shared with unauthorized parties. Any third-party services used to process personal information should be assessed and disclosed where appropriate.

8. Data Retention and Requests

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the intended purpose and applicable legal requirements. Requests to access, correct, or delete personal information may be made through the organization responsible for operating the system.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the system's features or data-handling practices change.
